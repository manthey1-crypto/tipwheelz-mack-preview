About Mr Wheelz

I'm Mr Wheelz, a Sacramento dad, gamer, and powerchair user who makes crude, comedy-first videos about disability and accessibility. My chair cost eighteen thousand dollars and still can't climb stairs, so I make fun of the places that think it should. Tips keep the videos coming and the fuckery documented. Every dollar helps, and none of it goes to stairs.
