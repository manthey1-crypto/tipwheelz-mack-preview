About TipWheelz

TipWheelz lets you voluntarily support Mr Wheelz’s comedy and accessibility work. By using the site or making a payment, you agree to these terms. Contact info@rollflow.net with questions.

Support here is for a creator. It is not a tax-deductible charitable donation.

One-time support

One-time options are charged once at the amount shown at checkout. No subscription is created for a one-time payment. Payment is processed through Stripe. Review the amount and email address before confirming; the email helps with receipts and support.

Monthly support

If you select monthly support, you authorize the amount shown at checkout ($3, $7, $15, or $30, depending on your choice) to be charged when you subscribe and automatically about every month thereafter until you cancel. The checkout must show your selected amount before you confirm. There is no free trial and no member perks promised for the monthly tiers.

You can cancel at any time through the Manage monthly support button on the site, using the same email address you subscribed with on any device. If that access is unavailable, email info@rollflow.net with your cancellation request. Cancellation stops future renewals; it does not automatically refund an already completed charge. Please cancel before the next renewal to prevent the next charge. We will tell you about any price changes before they take effect where required by law.

Sponsor credit

The $100 one-time Episode Sponsor option offers an optional 30-day end-card sponsor credit. We will coordinate the display name or credit with you by email. You may decline the credit. No other tier includes a sponsor credit or guaranteed creative output.

Support refunds and payment problems

If a charge looks mistaken, duplicated, or unauthorized, email info@rollflow.net promptly with the payment email and date so we can investigate. Completed voluntary support payments are not automatically refundable. Any rights you have under applicable law remain available. Do not send full card numbers by email.

Merchandise orders

Merchandise is a separate, one-time purchase, not a support contribution or subscription. Items are made to order by Printify and its print providers. Check the product, size, color, quantity, and delivery address before paying. Item prices, shipping charges, applicable tax, and the total are shown before payment. A payment confirmation is not a shipping confirmation.

Cancellations and changes

To request a cancellation or correction, email info@rollflow.net immediately with your order number and checkout email. If we confirm that the order has not been sent to production, we can cancel it and refund the cancelled items. A request alone does not cancel an order; wait for our confirmation. Production may begin shortly after payment, so there is no guaranteed cancellation or editing window.

Once an order has been sent to production, we do not accept change-of-mind cancellations, returns, exchanges, or refunds, including when you selected the wrong size or color. If our fulfillment partner can still stop the order, we will confirm whether an exception is possible. This restriction does not remove remedies for faulty or incorrect goods, non-delivery, or rights provided by law.

Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

If an item arrives damaged, has a manufacturing or printing defect, or is not what you ordered, contact us promptly with your order number, a description, and clear photos of the problem. Please report it within 30 calendar days of delivery so we can meet our fulfillment partner’s reporting deadline. We will review the issue and arrange an appropriate reprint, replacement, or refund. This reporting period does not limit rights that cannot lawfully be restricted. Do not return an item unless we give you return instructions.

Shipping problems and refunds

Production and delivery are separate stages, and items may ship separately. Check your delivery address carefully and contact us promptly about a missing, delayed, or undeliverable parcel. We review address and delivery problems individually. If we cannot ship within the promised time, we will offer the required delay notice and cancellation/refund options. If we cannot fulfill an order, we will refund the unfulfilled items and associated charges as required by law.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where possible, including applicable tax and shipping amounts where appropriate. Your bank or payment provider controls when the credit appears. A request to cancel monthly support does not cancel a merchandise order, and cancelling merchandise does not cancel monthly support.

Purchases on other platforms

This merchandise policy applies to orders paid directly through TipWheelz. If you complete a purchase on TikTok Shop or another marketplace, that platform’s purchase, return, and refund rules apply to that transaction and may provide additional rights. Contact us if you need help locating the right order.

Wall of Thanks and messages

The Wall of Thanks is optional. We display only a name you permitted us to show after a verified payment and review. We may decline or remove names that are abusive, impersonate another person, contain advertising, or otherwise cause harm. Notes sent during checkout are private messages to the TipWheelz team and are not posted on the public wall. Email info@rollflow.net to request a name change or removal.

Site use and content

Use the site lawfully and do not interfere with checkout or attempt unauthorized access. Site text, graphics, and Mr Wheelz content belong to their respective owners. A support payment does not grant rights to reuse that content or direct editorial decisions. Outside links lead to third-party services with their own terms.

Changes and availability

We may update site content, support options, and these terms. The terms in effect when you make a payment govern that payment, subject to applicable law. If the site or a payment service is unavailable, email us for help. Nothing in these terms removes rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

Contact

For billing, cancellation, privacy, or site questions, write to info@rollflow.net. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle supporter information.
